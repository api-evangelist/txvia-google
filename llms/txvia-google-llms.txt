# TxVia (Google)

> TxVia was a New York-based payments technology company providing an issuer-processing platform for prepaid and other emerging payment card programs. It was acquired by Google on April 2, 2012 and folded into Google Wallet (now Google Pay). TxVia no longer operates independently and publishes no developer program, public API, GitHub organization, or packages. txvia.com serves a Google-hosted acquisition notice.
